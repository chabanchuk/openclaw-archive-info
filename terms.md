# Terms of Use

Last updated: 27 October 2026

## Purpose

OpenClaw Archive rclone is a private backup utility operated by Serhii Chabanchuk for personal use. It is not offered as a public backup service.

## Authorized use

The utility is intended to access only Google accounts and files that its operator owns or is authorized to manage. Its use is subject to applicable Google terms and rclone licensing terms.

## Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for choosing backup contents, protecting credentials and encryption keys, managing storage capacity and retention, and verifying that backups can be restored.

## Availability

Successful transfers and uninterrupted availability are not guaranteed. Independent backup copies and periodic restore checks are necessary.

## Ending access

The operator may stop using the utility and revoke its access through Google Account settings. Existing backup files must be deleted separately if no longer needed.
